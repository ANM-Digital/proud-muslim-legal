Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Effective 2 September 2026. This combined document details our privacy practices and the terms governing your use of Proud Muslim.

Part I — Privacy Policy

Summary: Proud Muslim processes device permissions (such as location and notifications) locally to provide prayer times, Qibla direction, and reminders. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who We Are

Proud Muslim is provided by ANM Digital Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”). For privacy enquiries or data requests, contact us at info@anmdigital.online.

2. Information We Process

Location Data

With your explicit permission, the app processes precise or approximate GPS coordinates strictly on-device to calculate astronomical prayer times, identify your locality, and calculate Qibla direction. You may revoke location access at any time in device settings.

Device and Technical Information

Platform services may process basic technical data such as operating system version, device model, app version, language, and crash diagnostics to ensure app stability and security.

Local Preferences & Profile Data

User preferences (calculation method, juristic method, selected reciter, theme, bookmark progress, and profile image) are stored locally on your device storage.

Purchases & Subscriptions

In-app purchases and optional premium subscriptions are processed directly through Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not store or process your credit card numbers or banking information.

Customer Support Communications

When you email support, we retain your email address and message contents solely to resolve your support inquiry.

3. Device Permissions and Purposes

  • Location: Used strictly for calculating prayer times and Qibla compass bearing.
  • Compass & Motion Sensors: Used to dynamically orient the Qibla needle.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used to trigger adhan prayer calls and scheduled reminders.
  • Photo/Media Picker: Used solely if you voluntarily pick a custom profile picture.
  • Internet: Used to download Quran audio, fetch translation packages, and open official links.
  • Billing: Managed by the Google Play / App Store platform for optional upgrades.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

We process information based on contract performance (delivering requested app features), your explicit consent (for optional device permissions), legitimate interests in maintaining app security, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Platform infrastructure includes Google Play Services, Apple App Store, and operating-system notification channels. Each third party operates under its respective privacy terms.

6. Data Sharing & Non-Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

7. Data Retention

Local settings persist on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Support emails are retained only for as long as necessary to resolve inquiries.

8. International Transfers

Where technical platform providers operate globally, appropriate cross-border data protection safeguards are applied.

9. Children’s Privacy

Proud Muslim is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or applicable local age). Parents should supervise device permissions.

10. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security protocols to protect data. However, no digital system or internet transmission is 100% immune to risk.

11. Your Choices & Data Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, KVKK, and other privacy frameworks, you have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, and revoke device permissions. Contact info@anmdigital.online for inquiries.

12. User Rights & Data Deletion Instructions

You have full control over your personal data. You can delete your information using the following methods:

A. Removing Local Data

To delete all local preferences, bookmarks, custom calculation settings, and cached profile media, you can clear app storage in your device settings (Settings > Apps > Proud Muslim > Storage > Clear Data) or uninstall the app.

B. Server & Support Record Deletion

To delete support communications, email history, or customer service records held by ANM Digital Labs, email info@anmdigital.online with the subject 'Data Deletion Request'. We will verify and permanently delete your records within 30 days.

C. App Store Subscriptions

Uninstalling the app or clearing data does not automatically cancel app store subscriptions. To manage or cancel subscriptions, visit your Google Play Store or Apple App Store account settings.
