Voluntary Support

Support the Developer

Proud Muslim is developed and maintained independently. If you find the app useful and would like to support its continued development, you may make a voluntary contribution.

1. Voluntary Contributions

Supporting the developer is completely optional. You may choose to make a one-time or recurring contribution through the external support page linked below.

Important: A contribution does not unlock Premium features, digital content, subscriptions, additional functionality, or preferential treatment inside Proud Muslim.

2. Separate from Google Play Purchases

Voluntary developer support is separate from Proud Muslim Premium subscriptions and any other purchases processed through Google Play. Premium access, trials, renewals, cancellations, purchase restoration, and refund handling remain governed by the applicable Google Play purchase flow and Proud Muslim's published terms.

3. No Digital Goods in Exchange

Contributions are not payments for digital goods or services within the app. No Premium entitlement, content access, in-app currency, subscription period, feature access, or other digital benefit is granted in exchange for a contribution.

4. External Payment Provider

Contributions are processed outside the Proud Muslim app by an external payment provider. Proud Muslim does not collect or store your complete payment card details on this legal website.

Open Support Page →

5. Refunds and Payment Questions

Questions about a voluntary contribution should be directed through the payment provider's receipt, support, or transaction-resolution process, or by contacting Proud Muslim support at info@anmdigital.online. Google Play refund requests for app subscriptions or purchases remain separate from voluntary contributions.

6. Security

Never send passwords, full payment-card details, authentication codes, or government identification documents by email. Proud Muslim support will not request these credentials.
