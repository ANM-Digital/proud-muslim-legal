Important information about relying on Proud Muslim's religious and practical tools.

Not a religious ruling

Proud Muslim provides general educational and devotional tools. It does not issue fatwas, replace qualified scholarship, or represent any mosque, government ministry or religious authority unless expressly stated.

Prayer times and Hijri dates

Results depend on coordinates, time zone, calculation method, elevation, device clock and local conventions. High-latitude rules and local announcements may differ. Verify fasting, Eid, congregation and other important times locally.

Qibla direction

Compass readings can be affected by magnetic interference, cases, nearby electronics, buildings, calibration and sensor quality. Recalibrate your device and confirm the direction through another reliable method when necessary.

Quran, translations and religious texts

Translations are interpretations and may differ. Text, transliteration, audio, references or metadata can contain technical or source errors despite care. For study or rulings, consult a verified mushaf, authoritative edition and qualified scholar.

Zakat and financial tools

Any zakat calculation is an estimate and is not financial, tax, legal or religious advice. Asset treatment, nisab, debts, dates and valuation can differ. Seek qualified advice for your circumstances.

Notifications

Device settings, battery optimisation, permissions, operating-system restrictions or outages can delay or suppress reminders. Do not rely on the app as your only time-critical alert.
