Important Disclaimers

Disclaimer and Limitations

Crucial information regarding prayer times, Qibla compasses, translations, and religious advice.

Notice: Proud Muslim is an informational and technological worship tool. It does not replace qualified Islamic scholars, local religious authorities, or direct astronomical observation.

1. Prayer Time Limitations

Prayer times are calculated using recognized astronomical algorithms (e.g., Diyanet, MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura). Variations may arise due to elevation, atmospheric refraction, micro-climates, and local mosque conventions. Always follow your local mosque adhan when available.

2. Qibla & Compass Limitations

The digital Qibla compass depends on your mobile device’s internal magnetometer and GPS accuracy. Electromagnetic interference, metal cases, and uncalibrated sensors can cause directional deviations. Always calibrate your compass by moving your phone in a figure-8 motion.

3. Hijri Calendar Differences

Hijri dates are based on calculated astronomical moon phases. Actual Islamic months and religious observances (Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha) depend on verified local moonsighting declarations by official regional religious authorities.

4. Quran Translations & Interpretations

The authentic Quran is exclusively the Arabic text revealed to Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). All translations in English, Turkish, Urdu, German, etc., are human interpretations of the meanings and cannot encompass the full linguistic depth of the Arabic revelation.

5. Zakat Calculator Disclaimer

The Zakat tool provides general calculations based on user-entered values and standard nisab rates. Complex financial situations, business inventory, and specific debt obligations should be reviewed with a qualified Islamic financial scholar.

6. Notification Reliability

Device operating systems, battery optimization modes, Do Not Disturb settings, and OEM background task limits may delay or suppress adhan notifications. Users are advised not to rely exclusively on mobile notifications for time-critical obligations.

7. No Replacement for Islamic Scholars

No content within Proud Muslim constitutes formal legal fatwas. For personal fiqh, family law, or religious disputes, consult accredited Islamic scholars and authoritative institutions in your community.
