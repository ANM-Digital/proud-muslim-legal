Data Rights & Deletion

How to control app permissions, local data and privacy requests.

Control permissions

Use your device settings to grant or revoke location, notifications, alarms, photos/media and other permissions. Some features may stop working when a required permission is disabled.

Remove locally stored information

Proud Muslim stores certain preferences and feature state locally. You can remove this information by using an in-app reset option where available, clearing the app's storage in device settings, or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling does not cancel an app-store subscription.

Manage purchases

Use your Google Play or Apple account to view, cancel or manage subscriptions and purchase history. App-store records are controlled by the relevant platform and cannot be deleted by Proud Muslim.

Privacy request

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability for information controlled by ANM Digital Labs, email info@anmdigital.online with the subject “Proud Muslim Privacy Request.” Describe your request and the email address previously used to contact us. Do not send identity documents unless we specifically request a proportionate verification method.

What we may need

We may ask for limited information to verify the request, locate records and prevent unauthorised disclosure. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. Some information may be retained where required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or disputes.

Complaints

You may contact us first so we can address your concern. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
