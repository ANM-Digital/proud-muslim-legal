2. Prayer Calculation Methods & Astronomical Sources

Supported calculation methodologies include Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı (Turkey), Muslim World League (MWL), Islamic Society of North America (ISNA), Umm al-Qura University (Makkah), Egyptian General Authority of Survey, University of Islamic Sciences (Karachi), and Gulf Region conventions.

3. Hadith, Azkar & Supplication Sources

Adhkar, morning/evening supplications, and daily duas are sourced from Hisnul Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) by Saeed bin Ali bin Wahf Al-Qahtani, verified against authentic Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim narrations.

4. Audio & Media Sources

Quran audio recitation feeds are streamed from EveryAyah and Quranic audio archives featuring renowned reciters (Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Abdul Basit Abdul Samad, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary). Adhan recordings represent authentic calls to prayer from Makkah, Madinah, and Al-Aqsa.

Verification Policy: We do not invent sources or licenses. All dataset information is cross-referenced with upstream maintainers. If any discrepancy is identified, it is promptly reviewed and corrected.
