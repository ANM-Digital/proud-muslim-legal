About Proud Muslim

A faith-focused companion designed to support everyday Islamic practice.

Proud Muslim is a mobile application developed by ANM Digital Labs. It brings practical Islamic tools together in one respectful, accessible experience.

What the app offers

  • Prayer times and configurable prayer reminders
  • Qibla direction tools using device location and sensors
  • Quran reading and translations
  • Daily adhkar, hadith and verse content
  • Digital tasbih, Hijri calendar and zakat tools
  • The 99 Names of Allah with text and audio features
  • Theme, language and accessibility-oriented preferences

Our approach

We aim to make core religious reading and listening features—including Quran content, the 99 Names of Allah and adhan listening—available without compromising their dignity. Some optional or expanded app services may be offered through subscriptions or purchases where clearly disclosed in the app.

Important note

Proud Muslim is a practical aid, not a mosque, scholar, mufti, legal adviser or authoritative religious body. Users should consult qualified local authorities where accuracy or religious rulings are important.
