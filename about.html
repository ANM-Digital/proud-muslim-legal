About Proud Muslim

Empowering Muslims worldwide with accurate prayer times, Quran study, authentic adhkar, and Islamic tools.

1. Our Purpose

Proud Muslim is an all-in-one Islamic utility application designed to assist Muslims in their daily worship, Quranic recitation, prayer punctuality, and Islamic learning with a clean, respectful interface.

We prioritize privacy, accuracy, accessibility, and respect for Islamic traditions while leveraging modern mobile technology.

2. Main Application Features

Proud Muslim provides a rich suite of daily Islamic tools, including:

  • Accurate Prayer Times with worldwide calculation methods and customizable adhan notifications.
  • Qibla Direction finder using onboard device compass and GPS sensors.
  • Complete Holy Quran with multi-language translations, word-by-word highlights, and audio recitations.
  • 99 Names of Allah (Asma-ul-Husna) with audio pronunciations, meanings, and comprehensive explanations.
  • Hisnul Muslim Adhkar, Duas, and digital Tasbih counter for daily remembrance.
  • Islamic Hijri Calendar, Zakat Calculator, and Community Mosque locator tools.

3. Free-Forever Core Commitment

Commitment: The Holy Quran text, 99 Names of Allah, and fundamental prayer adhans will always remain 100% accessible to every user.

We firmly believe that essential Islamic worship tools should be freely accessible. Core Quran reading, audio adhans for prayer calls, and Asma-ul-Husna study will never be locked behind mandatory paywalls.

4. Developer Information

Proud Muslim is developed, published, and maintained by ANM Digital Labs. We are dedicated to creating high-quality, privacy-respecting software and digital tools for the global Muslim community.

5. Religious Information Limitation

Proud Muslim is an informational and technological aid. It does not issue religious fatwas, replace qualified Islamic scholars, or substitute local mosque authorities. Users should always consult qualified scholars for important fiqh and religious rulings.
