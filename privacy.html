Privacy Policy

Effective 2 September 2026. This policy explains how Proud Muslim handles information.

Summary: Proud Muslim uses device permissions to provide location-based prayer times, Qibla direction, reminders, profile images and purchases. Some preferences and content may be stored locally. Network requests and platform services may process technical information.

1. Who we are

Proud Muslim is provided by ANM Digital Labs (“we”, “us”). Privacy enquiries may be sent to info@anmdigital.online.

2. Information the app may process

Location

With permission, the app may process precise or approximate location to calculate prayer times, identify a relevant place and determine Qibla direction. You can deny or revoke location access in device settings and may use manual location options where available.

Device and app information

The app and its platform providers may process device type, operating-system version, app version, language, time zone, diagnostics, network information and purchase status as needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

Preferences and user-provided content

Settings such as language, theme, prayer calculation preferences, notification choices, counters and selected profile images may be stored on your device. If you choose an image, the app accesses only the media you select through the operating-system picker, subject to platform behaviour.

Purchases

Subscriptions or purchases are processed by the applicable app store. We do not receive your full payment-card details. We may receive transaction identifiers, product, entitlement, status and related purchase metadata needed to provide and restore access.

Communications

If you contact us, we process the contact details and message contents you provide so we can respond and keep appropriate support records.

3. Permissions and purposes

  • Location: prayer times, place selection and Qibla direction.
  • Compass and motion sensors: orientation and Qibla functionality.
  • Notifications, vibration, exact alarms and start-up: prayer and other reminders requested by you.
  • Photos/media picker: selecting a profile image or other user-requested media.
  • Internet: retrieving permitted content, fonts or data; opening links; and supporting platform services.
  • Billing: processing and restoring app-store purchases.

4. Legal bases

Depending on your location, we rely on performance of a service you request, your consent (especially for optional device permissions), our legitimate interests in securing and improving the app, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Service providers

Relevant providers may include Google Play or Apple App Store for distribution and billing, operating-system providers for permissions and notifications, and content/data providers requested by the app. Their own policies govern their independent processing. The precise provider set may change as the app develops; material changes will be reflected in this policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers acting for us, app-store/platform providers, professional advisers, authorities where legally required, or a successor in a corporate transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention

Local settings remain until you reset the app, clear its storage or uninstall it. Purchase records are retained by app stores under their policies. Support communications are kept only as reasonably needed for support, security, disputes and legal obligations.

8. International transfers

Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use or rely on recognised transfer safeguards.

9. Children

Proud Muslim is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use and manage device permissions and purchases.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no device, transmission or storage method is completely secure.

11. Your choices and rights

You can control permissions in device settings, control notifications in the app or device settings, and remove local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Depending on applicable law, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability. See Data Rights.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as features, providers or laws change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

13. Contact

Email info@anmdigital.online. Please do not send sensitive religious, health, financial or identity information unless necessary.
